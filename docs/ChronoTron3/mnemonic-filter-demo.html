mnemonic — K4 / K5 converging-band filter

Proposed model for confirmation. Two 24 dB/oct (4-pole) filters — a high-pass and a low-pass — define a passband. K4 sets where the delay lives (tilt / center); K5 reduces the space between the HP and LP cutoffs, narrowing the band. No resonant band-pass peak — the emphasis comes from the two cutoffs converging.
